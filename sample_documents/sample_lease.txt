RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2025, by and between:

LANDLORD: Greenwood Properties LLC, a California limited liability company ("Landlord")
TENANT: Jane Doe ("Tenant")

PROPERTY: 1234 Maple Street, Apt 5B, San Francisco, California 94102 ("Premises")

GOVERNING LAW: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California.

1. TERM
The lease term shall commence on February 1, 2025, and shall end on January 31, 2026 ("Lease Term"). This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-month periods unless either party provides written notice of termination at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2. RENT
Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Two Thousand Eight Hundred Dollars ($2,800.00). Rent is due on the first (1st) day of each month. A late fee of Seventy-Five Dollars ($75.00) per day shall be assessed for each day rent remains unpaid after the fifth (5th) day of the month. Rent shall be paid via electronic funds transfer to Landlord's designated account.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Five Thousand Six Hundred Dollars ($5,600.00) — equivalent to two months' rent — upon execution of this Agreement. The deposit may be applied by Landlord to cover unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, cleaning costs, or any other amounts owed by Tenant. The deposit shall be returned within twenty-one (21) days of Tenant's vacating the Premises, subject to any lawful deductions.

4. UTILITIES AND SERVICES
Tenant shall be solely responsible for and shall timely pay all charges for gas, electricity, internet, cable television, and telephone services. Landlord shall pay for water, trash collection, and building common-area maintenance. If Tenant fails to maintain any required utility service and such failure causes damage to the Premises, Tenant shall be liable for all resulting repair costs.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall maintain the Premises in a clean, sanitary, and undamaged condition. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord in writing of any needed repairs. Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs caused by Tenant's negligence or misuse, including but not limited to damages caused by pets, guests, or subtenants. Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, plumbing, heating, and electrical systems, provided Tenant notifies Landlord within forty-eight (48) hours of discovery of any such issue.

6. ALTERATIONS
Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Any permitted alterations shall become the property of Landlord upon termination of this Agreement unless Landlord directs Tenant in writing to restore the Premises to its original condition, in which case Tenant shall bear all restoration costs.

7. SUBLETTING
Tenant shall not sublet all or any portion of the Premises, nor assign this Agreement, without Landlord's prior written consent, which may be withheld at Landlord's sole discretion.

8. PETS
No pets of any kind shall be kept on the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. If consent is granted, Tenant shall pay an additional non-refundable pet fee of Five Hundred Dollars ($500.00) per pet and an additional monthly pet rent of One Hundred Dollars ($100.00) per pet.

9. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
Landlord may enter the Premises upon twenty-four (24) hours written notice to Tenant for the purpose of inspection, repairs, or showing the unit to prospective tenants. In the event of emergency, Landlord may enter without prior notice.

10. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement at the end of the initial Lease Term or any renewal period by providing thirty (30) days written notice. If Tenant vacates the Premises before the end of the Lease Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall remain liable for rent through the end of the Lease Term or until the Premises are re-let, whichever occurs first. Landlord shall make reasonable efforts to re-let the Premises.

11. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES
If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, or violates any other term of this Agreement, Landlord may serve written notice requiring Tenant to cure the default within three (3) days. If Tenant fails to cure within the cure period, Landlord may pursue all remedies available under California law, including unlawful detainer proceedings. In any action arising from this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees.

12. INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord and its members, managers, agents, and employees from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including attorneys' fees) arising from or relating to: (a) Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises; (b) any act, omission, or negligence of Tenant, Tenant's household members, guests, or pets; or (c) any breach by Tenant of this Agreement. Landlord shall not be liable for any loss or damage to Tenant's personal property.

13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
In no event shall Landlord be liable to Tenant for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to loss of business, loss of profits, or inconvenience, even if Landlord has been advised of the possibility of such damages.

14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising from this Agreement shall first be submitted to non-binding mediation. If mediation fails, the dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its then-current rules. The arbitration shall be conducted in San Francisco, California. THE PARTIES WAIVE THEIR RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL AND TO PARTICIPATE IN A CLASS ACTION.

15. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, warranties, and understandings.
